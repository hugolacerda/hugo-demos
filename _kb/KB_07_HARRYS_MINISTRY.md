# Light to the Nations Ministry — Project Reference

*Created: March 2026*
*Status: Active — initial assessment phase*

---

## Overview

This is a separate engagement from GBA/SBV work. Gary Hallett connected Hugo with
a long-time friend named Harry who runs a Christian ministry in Guatemala called
Light to the Nations (formerly Light of the World Ministries). Harry has no
technology skills and no resources to manage his own web presence or digital
infrastructure. The goal is to help Harry regain control of his technology, rebuild
his online presence, and eventually connect his ministry with US churches that could
use his Guatemala facility as a mission base camp.

This is funded separately from GBA/SBV hours. Gary's men's group member Morris has
committed to covering some of Hugo's hours monthly for this project. Additional
funding may come from other men's group members over time as traction builds.

---

## The Ministry

**Name:** Light to the Nations (formerly Light of the World Ministries)
**Location:** Guatemala (specific location not documented yet)
**Focus:** Christian outreach, English language classes for Spanish-speaking
Guatemalans, and hosting visiting mission teams from the US and elsewhere.

**Facility:** Harry built a two-story worship center of roughly 12,000-15,000 sq ft
on a small piece of property he owns. He built it from plans he carried in his head
with no documentation on paper. The building includes: multiple bedrooms plus a
bunk-bed room for hosting teams, a large commercial kitchen, and an upstairs worship
center. The facility regularly hosts visiting teams who use it as a base camp for
outreach in the surrounding community.

**Key activity:** English language classes. Harry and his team teach English to
local Hispanic residents. Rudy participated as a guest teacher when he visited.

**Current reach:** A team from Peru was in residence as of late March 2026. The
facility functions as a base camp model, where outside teams come in, stay, and
conduct outreach from there.

## Current Situation

Harry has no meaningful control over his digital presence. Shelly managed the
ministry's website, Facebook, financial accounts, and communication channels before
their separation. When she left, she took access to all of it. Harry is left with
whatever remains, which Gary describes as minimal and disorganized.

Harry has no money. A small group of supporters, including Gary, Morris, and a local
bank president, provide financial support informally. Many days the ministry does not
have enough money for food on the table.

A local contact in Guatemala had been working on a website for Harry but Gary
expected it would be low quality. The ministry's name change from Light of the World
to Light to the Nations has added to the confusion around online identity.

---

## Initial Scope of Work

Gary outlined three priority areas when introducing this project:

1. **Get control over what Harry has.** Audit his existing digital assets: website,
   domain registration, Facebook, email, and any other accounts Shelly may have
   managed. Understand what Hugo has access to and what is locked or missing.

2. **Assess and stabilize the web presence.** Evaluate whatever exists and determine
   what needs to be rebuilt vs. what can be recovered. The name change from Light of
   the World to Light to the Nations also needs to be reflected consistently.

3. **Set up for church outreach.** The longer-term goal is to position the ministry
   so US churches can find it, understand the value of using the Guatemala facility
   as a base camp, and begin financially supporting the work. This likely involves
   a website that tells the story clearly, a donation mechanism, and outreach to
   mission teams inside churches.
