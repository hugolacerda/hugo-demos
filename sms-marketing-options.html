How people say yes

A checkbox on sign-up

An optional box on the workshop form. Already built for reminders; we add a second one.

A QR code at your talks

Last slide or table card: "Scan to be invited to the next workshop." Fits how you meet clients.

Text a word to our number

"Text WORKSHOP to 904-674-8478." Easy to say out loud from a stage or a podcast.

What we would not do

Text cold lists

Not allowed, and it can get our number shut down.

Frequent promos to sellers

Selling a business is a slow, private decision. Email and calls fit better.
