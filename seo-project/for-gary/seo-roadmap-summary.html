Lighthouse's own bands: 90 to 100 good · 50 to 89 needs work · 0 to 49 poor.

A note on that SEO score of 100. Lighthouse's SEO check is a short list of basics: does each page have a title, can Google read it, do the links work. SBV already passed those before we started. It doesn't look at whether the site answers what owners search for, how SBV compares with competitors, or what other sites say about SBV, and those decide most of where a site ranks. A perfect score means the foundation is sound, not that the SEO work is done.
