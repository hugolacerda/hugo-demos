The question you asked

How do you take a company from barely using AI to running on it?

You put it plainly on the call. Most business owners are stuck on the bottom rung, typing questions into ChatGPT and getting a little done. The real prize is much further up: a business where AI agents handle the repeatable work and the owner just approves the output.

The question is how we walk a company from the bottom of that ladder to the point where it actually runs differently. Here is how I would answer that.

The ladder

Where companies are, and where we take them

Steve laid out nine rungs on the webinar. You do not need all nine to see the opportunity. These are the ones that matter.

1

5
An operating systemThe target
Agents that run real work in sequence, with a person approving the result.

Our service takes a company from rung 1 to rung 5. Most of the people on that webinar understood the idea. Almost none of them could actually build it. That gap is the whole opportunity.

What it is

The service in one sentence

We go into a business, find the repeatable work that eats their people's time, and build them a supervised team of AI agents that does it.

That is the whole product. Not their website. Not their social media. Not their email marketing. If a client needs those, it is a separate job we add on or hand off. Keeping this narrow is the point. Anyone can call themselves an AI agency. Almost no one can actually build a working agent system. That is the thing we can do.

The one principle that makes it work

We standardize the approach, not the agents

You made this point yourself on the call, and it is the key to the whole thing. In the old world, customizing for every client was expensive, and as you pointed out, it even lowers a company's score on the Value Builder assessment.

Agents flip that. We can build something custom for each client cheaply. A recast agent we build for SBV is useless to a plumbing company. But the method for finding what to automate and building the agents is the same every time. The agents are custom and cheap. The method is the thing we reuse and sell.

The engagement

What it looks like, step by step

You asked for this directly. What is the process, how long does it take, what does the client have to do, when do they see something. Here is the shape of it.

1
Your front door · 1 to 2 weeks
The Audit
What happens

We sit with the business and map how work actually moves through it. We find the repeatable, document-heavy tasks eating people's time, and we rank them by what they are worth to fix.

What the client gets

A clear map of what AI can take off their plate and what each piece is worth in time or money.

Why it matters

This is a paid engagement on its own. It is low risk for the client, it is where we earn their trust, and it tells us exactly what to build next. Companies buy assessments all day long. This is the easiest yes to get.

2
A few days
The Plan
What happens

We take the best opportunities from the audit and decide exactly which agents we are building. We set the timeline, what the client has to give us, and when the first draft is ready.

Why it matters

This is the part you said we cannot hand wave. We have to walk in and say here is how it works, here is how long it takes, here is what we need from you. No "it depends on the client."

3
Depends on scope
The Build
What happens

This is the part we already know how to do. We build each agent the same way we are building the SBV team, one job at a time, tested and tightened over a few runs until it handles the work cleanly.

What the client gives us

Their know-how, and an example of the output they want, the same way Nancy gives us a sample report to aim at.

What the client gets

Working agents that do a real piece of their business.

4
The open question
The Handoff
What happens

The agents have to live somewhere the client can actually use without me setting up their computer every time. This is the hard part, and I am flagging it as the biggest thing still to figure out.

Why it matters

This decides whether this is a service I run by hand for a handful of clients, or a real product we can sell at scale. Flint has already solved a version of this with his browser agents. That, plus owning the AI account on our end, is the likely path. We design this here.

5
Monthly
Ongoing
What happens

We watch the agents, tune them, and build new ones as the business finds more to automate.

Why it matters

This is the recurring revenue, and it is what keeps us in the seat when the owner eventually decides to sell. That is the tie back to GBA.

How we would charge

Three pieces, same shape as SBV

I am not putting dollar figures on this yet. The real numbers come out of the SBV build, because that is the only place we will have honest data on how long things take, and the pricing is your call regardless. The shape, though, looks like this.

The AuditFixed fee
A flat price for the opportunity map. The front door, and the easiest sale.
The BuildProject fee
Priced to how many agents we are building. Scoped from the audit so there are no surprises.
OngoingMonthly
For keeping it running and growing it over time. The recurring revenue.

The logic is the same funnel you already believe in. The audit sells the build. The build sells the monthly.

Why we can sell this

SBV is the proof, not a hurdle

The worry is that we cannot sell this until the SBV agents are finished. Flip it around. The SBV pipeline is not something we have to clear before the service exists. It is the service, run once, on our own company.

It proves the one thing that matters: that we can walk into a business, find the work worth automating, and build a system that does it. So yes, SBV needs to be live and working as our first real example. That is very different from saying the idea is unproven. We are not missing a piece. We are finishing our proof.

Who buys it first

Our valuation clients are the warm list

The easiest people to sell this to are owners who already went through an SBV valuation. We already know their numbers. We already know where their business is weak and where the owner is the bottleneck.

That is the exact cross-sell you were circling on the call. The third service lives inside "Grow the Value." We do not have to sell a full transformation to a stranger. We sell the audit. The audit sells the rest.
