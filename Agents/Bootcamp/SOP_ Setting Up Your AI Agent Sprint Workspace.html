Setting Up Your AI Agent Sprint Workspace

Four steps. Fifteen minutes. Create your Operate folder, set up the value chain folders that mirror how your business operates, build the GAAPC structure inside each one, and point Claude at it. By the end you will have an organized workspace with a Chief of Staff operating system on top.

Step 01

Create Your Operate Folder

Create a folder called Operate at the top level of your user directory. On Mac that is your home folder — the same level as Desktop, Documents, and Downloads. On Windows, put it inside your user folder.

This is your single workspace. Everything you do with Claude lives here. One folder, one source of truth. Do not scatter files across Desktop, Downloads, or random project folders. Operate is home base.

Mac: ~/Operate. Windows: C:\Users\YourName\Operate. Keep it at the top level so it is easy to find.

Step 02

Set Up the Value Chain

As a CEO, your folders are not personal responsibilities — they are the functional areas of the business. The structure mirrors how your company actually operates, from strategy through continuous improvement.

Folder 0 is the Chief of Staff. This is the operating system — it sits on top of everything else and orients every work session. Folders 1 through 10 follow the company value chain. The numbers keep them sorted and give Claude a predictable structure to navigate.

Create all eleven folders. The Chief of Staff folder is where your operating rules, kanban, and session context live. Every other folder maps to a functional area of the business.

Step 03

Build GAAPC Inside Each One

Every responsibility folder gets the same five subfolders. The mnemonic is GAAPC. This structure separates what you are trying to achieve from how you do the work from the work itself.

Goals — what you are trying to achieve in this area. Advisors — strategic guidance for how to think about this domain. Agents — repeatable processes and SOPs. Projects — the actual work, each in its own folder. Context — reference material, data, background, signal, and shipped work.

You do not need to fill every subfolder on day one. The structure is the scaffold. The content builds over time as you work.

Step 05

How the Folders Work Day to Day

Every time you start a new task in Cowork, Claude creates a project folder inside the right responsibility area. The project lives there from start to finish.

Over time, the pattern compounds. Goals tell Claude what matters. Advisors tell it how to think. Agents give it repeatable processes. Projects are where the work happens. Context feeds background, signal, and shipped work back in.

The folders are empty scaffolding on day one. By week four they are full of context that makes every new project faster.
