The End Game

The ultimate business is the
intelligence system.

None of these productized services are the ultimate business. They get you in the door. The real play is becoming the fractional elite AI operator — the person who runs the intelligence of the company.

01
Give them agents. They create the problem you need them to have. They’ll try to use them. No coherent system. No context organized.
02
Build the operating system. Context is one of the main folders. You teach them how to fill it. Without it, nothing works.
03
Become the fractional elite AI operator. All work goes through you. Every deliverable gets analyzed by you. You’re in charge of the intelligence of the company.
04
Build a network of intelligence. Multiple clients = compounding insight. You bring intelligence from all the companies you work with. If they stop working with you, that goes away.
