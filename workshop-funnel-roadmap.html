Decision needed

What should the follow-up emails ask people to do?

After the workshop we can point people at the recording, at the Value Builder scorecard, or at both. Right now both follow-up sequences are paused so we don't build them twice, once now and once after your call. Below are the three ways it could go, each shown as the full email the person would actually receive.

Attendees got, before pausing

The scorecard only

No-shows would get, once switched on

The recording and the scorecard

OPTION A

The scorecard only

Real email, sent to attendees before pausing

One ask, nothing competing with it. This is the strongest pull toward the scorecard, which is the step that actually starts a conversation. The trade-off is that anyone who wanted to watch a replay first has nowhere to go.

OPTION B

The recording only

Mockup, not built yet

The easiest yes, and the softest ask. Good for someone who has gone cold, since watching a video asks almost nothing of them. The cost is that the scorecard moves into the video description, one click further away, where far fewer people will find it.

OPTION C

Both, recording first

Real email, ready to send to no-shows

What the no-show emails say today. Covers both intentions in one send: catch up on what you missed, then go a step further. The usual risk with two asks in one email is that fewer people do either one.

The question: keep the split as it is, with attendees getting the scorecard alone and no-shows getting both? Or make both groups get the same thing?

There is a reasonable case for the current split. Attendees already saw the content, so a recording link is redundant for them, while a no-show has nothing yet and the recording is the only thing that catches them up. If that logic holds for you, this just needs a yes and we switch both sequences back on. Either way, nothing else changes, this only affects email wording.
