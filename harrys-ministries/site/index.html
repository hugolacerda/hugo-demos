Where a US church sends a team, not just a check

Luz a las Naciones is a mission center in Poptún, Guatemala, founded by Harry in 2018. It houses visiting teams, feeds neighbors, and teaches English to a community that keeps showing up.

What we do · Index 01-04

Four things happen here

English classes for local families, a yearly gift drive, meals and a bed for missionaries passing through, and a girls' program built around identity and courage. A visiting team can usually plug into at least one of them directly.
