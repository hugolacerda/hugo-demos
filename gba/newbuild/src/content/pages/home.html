WhyGateway Business Advisors

Most brokers want to list your business. We want to sell it. 

Gateway sits between business brokers and investment bankers. We bring the discipline, process, and judgment of an investment banker to businesses that a traditional broker would simply list and hope for the best.

We work with a limited number of clients at any given time. That is a deliberate choice. It means every owner we engage gets our full attention, a professional process, and a realistic assessment of what is possible.

We do not take every listing. If your business is not positioned to sell well, we will tell you that honestly. And if you have time, we will help you change it.

Key Differentiators:

  • We work with a limited number of clients at a time, never more than three active engagements
  • We assess sellability before going to market, so owners know what buyers will see
  • We run a competitive sale process designed to attract multiple buyers and optimize price, terms, and conditions
  • We connect owners to the right resources across valuation, advisory, legal, and financing when needed
  • We are honest about timing. A few months of preparation often produces a far better outcome than rushing to market
