The Business Sales Process

1. Discovery

  • Initial Conversation
  • 360 Assessment
  • Present Findings
  • Next Steps

2. Preparation

  • Engagement Agreement | Retainer
  • Business Analysis, Interview
  • Marketing Materials
  • Target Buyer Search

3. Marketing

  • Confidential Outreach
  • Selected Online Placement
  • Buyer Screening

4. Buyer Management

  • Initial Contact, Pre-qualification
  • Secure Non-Disclosure Agreement
  • Schedule Buyer-Seller Calls/Meetings
  • Ensure Timely Submission of Letters of Intent Negotiate/Accept Final LOI

5. Getting to close

  • Kick Off Meeting to Begin Process
  • Coordinate Due Diligence, Data Room and Communication
  • Remove Contingencies
  • Negotiate Final Purchase and Sale Agreement
  • Schedule Closing, Coordinate Last Minute Items
  • Close the Deal!
