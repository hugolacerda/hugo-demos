Start here

Think of this - your business as an asset, possibly your most-valuable asset. When it’s time to capitalize on what you’ve built and move on to the next phase of your life’s journey, you will most likely want to convert your asset into cash and income-producing investments that will fund your desired lifestyle.We say START HERE because the outcome you hope to achieve is directly connected to the business you own - the financial value of that business and most importantly, why it’s a compelling investment for prospective purchasers.

To put it simply, Selling Your Business is a journey not a destination, and the journey begins with  DISCOVERY.

Disclaimer: When we use the words Sell or Selling, we’re really talking about transitioning out of your business. You might want to transition to family or to a key employee, and that’s OK. Regardless of what the transition looks like, it is our goal to help you maximize that financial value of your business and after-sale proceeds in that transition.

DISCOVERY

A poorly planned exit from your business could cause you to end up in a place wildly different from where you expected, exhausted and disappointed. You want to avoid that, we want you to avoid that. With the advantage of history and experience, our firm guides business owners into the unknown with a map and process to provide you with the most accurate information from which you can make informed decisions. 

The process begins with DISCOVERY, an opportunity for us to learn about each other, assess your business value, industry dynamics and market conditions. Upon completion of DISCOVERY, you will have a strong understanding of the Business Sales Process and options to consider for your business and personal consideration.
