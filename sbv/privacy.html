Our principal office is located at 5011 Gate Parkway, Jacksonville, FL 32256. We can be reached at info@strategicbusinessvaluations.com or (904) 759-7902.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Contact forms: When you submit an inquiry through our website, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and the details of your message.
  • Event registrations: When you register for a webinar or workshop, we collect your name, email address, and any information you provide in the registration form.
  • Consultations: When you schedule or attend a discovery call, we may collect information about your business, including revenue, ownership structure, and transition timeline.
  • Website analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymized information about how visitors use our site, including pages visited, time on site, and referral sources.
  • Advertising: We use Meta (Facebook) advertising tools, which may collect information about your interactions with our ads and website through cookies and tracking pixels.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and schedule consultations
  • Deliver webinars, workshops, and educational resources you have registered for
  • Send follow-up communications related to services you have expressed interest in
  • Improve our website, services, and marketing based on aggregate usage data
  • Comply with applicable legal and professional obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for any purpose materially different from those described above without your consent.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies, including:

  • Google Analytics: Measures traffic and usage patterns. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.
  • Meta Pixel: Tracks conversions from Facebook and Instagram ads and enables us to show you relevant advertising on those platforms.
  • GoHighLevel CRM: Our customer relationship management system may set session cookies to support form submissions and contact tracking.
